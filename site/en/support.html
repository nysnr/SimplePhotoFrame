SimplePhotoFrame Support

Last updated: 2026-03-24

Thank you for using SimplePhotoFrame. On this page you'll find basic support information, a quick start guide, frequently asked questions, and the developer contact details for the app listed on the App Store under ASC App ID 6792314431.

Quick Start

  1. Install SimplePhotoFrame from the App Store on your iPad or iPhone (iOS 15.1 or later).
  2. Open the app and grant permission to access your photo library when prompted.
  3. Select the photos you want to appear in the slideshow.
  4. Tap Start Slideshow. Adjust interval, matte color, clock/date display, and language in Settings.
  5. Prop your device on a stand, dock, or tripod — and enjoy your sustainable photo frame.

Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of app is this?

SimplePhotoFrame is a simple photo frame app that displays photos stored on your device as a slideshow, with optional clock and date display. It was built to help reuse older iPads and iPhones rather than letting them go to e-waste.

Does the app upload my photos?

No. Your photos stay on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. All slideshow processing happens locally on-device.

Why are ads not displayed?

Ad display may depend on region, network conditions, account review status, or ad inventory availability. Banner ads support ongoing maintenance of the app.

Can I use an older iPad as a photo frame?

Yes. Many users use SimplePhotoFrame to reuse an older or unused iPad or iPhone as a digital clock photo frame. The UI is minimal and the app uses light image caching, so older hardware typically runs it smoothly.

Which iOS versions are supported?

iOS 15.1 and later. Supported on iPad and iPhone.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings inside the app and choose one of the four supported languages: Japanese, English, Spanish, or Chinese Simplified.
