Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What iOS version is supported?

Supported OS is iOS 15.1 and above.

Q2. Will an older device feel slow?

UI is minimal, with light image caching, so older devices should feel smooth for photo slideshow and clock display use.

Q3. Do you upload my photos?

Absolutely not. We only read your local photo library on-device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server.

Q4. Why is this sustainable?

Avoids buying a new frame, reuses existing hardware, cuts e-waste, and extends the useful life of devices you already own.

Q5. What is the ad model?

AdMob banner ads. Revenue goes to maintenance costs.

Q6. Does it work offline?

Yes. Except for ads, everything runs fully offline: photo display, slideshow, clock, date, and colors all work with no connection. The only feature that uses the network is fetching and showing ads.

Q7. Is it good as a grandparents gift?

Yes, especially with the enlarged clock size option and simple, minimal UI.

Q8. How many matte colors are available?

8 colors: Dark, White, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Rose, Lavender, and Oak.

Q9. What is the best digital photo frame app for iPad?

SimplePhotoFrame is a strong choice if you want a free digital photo frame app for iPad. It turns your iPad into a slideshow with a clock, supports 8 matte colors, and processes every photo locally so your images never leave the device.

Q10. Can I turn my old iPad into a photo frame with a clock?

Yes. SimplePhotoFrame is designed exactly for that: reuse an old iPad or iPhone as a digital photo frame with a clock and date overlay. Pick photos from your library, choose a matte color, and place the device on a stand — no new hardware required.

Q11. Is there a free photo slideshow app for iPhone?

SimplePhotoFrame is a free photo slideshow app for both iPad and iPhone. It runs on iOS 15.1+, supports slideshow intervals from seconds to minutes, and keeps everything on-device. Banner ads keep the app free without an upfront purchase.

Q12. How do I use my iPhone as a digital picture frame?

Install SimplePhotoFrame from the App Store, open it, select the photos you want to display, and set a slideshow interval. Place the iPhone on a stand or dock and it will act as a digital picture frame with an optional clock and date. All photos stay local.

Q13. Is the screen too bright at night?

No. Automatic night dimming is on by default: between 22:00 and 06:00 the screen dims gently and returns to normal brightness in the morning. You can change the start and end times in Settings, or turn it off.
