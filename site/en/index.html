The Problem

Unused iPads and iPhones stored away

Millions of perfectly working devices are left in drawers, gathering dust instead of being loved.

Old devices heading for e-waste

Upgrades often mean perfectly functional devices end up in landfills, leaking heavy metals into the environment.

Dedicated photo frames are costly

Purpose-built digital frames can cost $80–$200 — yet you probably already own a perfectly good display at home.

Environmental impact of electronics waste

E-waste is the fastest growing waste stream globally. Reusing hardware is one of the highest-impact choices we can make.

Privacy & Local Processing

We NEVER upload your photos to any server — all processing happens locally on-device

SimplePhotoFrame only reads the photos you select from your photo library. The images stay on your iPad or iPhone, and no network upload ever occurs.

Works fully offline

Once the app is installed, the slideshow, clock, and all settings work completely offline. You can place the frame anywhere — no Wi-Fi required after setup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What iOS version is supported?

Supported OS is iOS 15.1 and above.

Q2. Will an older device feel slow?

UI is minimal, with light image caching, so older devices should feel smooth for photo slideshow and clock display use.

Q3. Do you upload my photos?

Absolutely not. We only read your local photo library on-device. Your photos are never uploaded to any server.

Q4. Why is this sustainable?

Avoids buying a new frame, reuses existing hardware, cuts e-waste, and extends the useful life of devices you already own.

Q5. What is the ad model?

AdMob banner ads. Revenue goes to maintenance costs.

Q6. Does it work offline?

Yes, core features work fully offline. All photo processing happens locally on your device.

Q7. Is it good as a grandparents gift?

Yes, especially with the enlarged clock size option and simple, minimal UI.

Q8. How many matte colors are available?

8 colors: Dark, White, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Rose, Lavender, and Oak.
